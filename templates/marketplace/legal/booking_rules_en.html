Rules for Booking Services and Facilities Using the iBron Service

1. General Provisions

1.1. These Rules govern the procedure for booking services, appointment times, workstations, equipment, premises and other bookable facilities through the iBron.uz website and services.

1.2. The iBron service provides Users with the technical ability to:

  • find Venues and Professionals;
  • view available services, prices and schedules;
  • select a date and time for a visit;
  • submit a booking request;
  • receive notifications regarding the status of a booking;

manage their bookings.

1.3. The Platform may include, among others:

  • hair salons and barbershops;
  • beauty salons;
  • manicure, pedicure, massage and other service Professionals;
  • computer clubs;
  • billiard clubs;
  • padel courts and other sports facilities;

other organisations and Professionals providing services by appointment or reservation.

1.4. iBron is an online information platform and, unless expressly stated otherwise, does not independently provide the services listed on the Platform.

1.5. The service agreement is entered into directly between the User and the relevant Venue, Professional or other Service Provider.

1.6. Submission of a booking request does not always constitute final confirmation of the booking. If the selected Venue uses manual confirmation, the booking shall be deemed confirmed only after its status has been changed to “Confirmed” and the relevant notification has been sent to the User.

2. Requirements for Services and Bookable Facilities

2.1. The Venue or Professional must provide the service or booked facility in accordance with the information specified on the Platform, including:

  • the type and name of the service;
  • the duration of the service or rental;
  • the price;
  • the selected date and time;
  • the address where the service will be provided;
  • information about the Professional or specialist;

the characteristics of the workstation, court, table, computer, room or other facility;

  • the conditions of attendance, rental and use;
  • the photographs and description displayed on the Venue’s page;

the stated amenities, equipment and additional features.

2.2. The Venue must ensure that the following information remains accurate and up to date:

  • schedules;
  • available time slots;
  • prices;
  • Professionals;
  • available services;
  • workstations and bookable facilities;
  • cancellation policies;
  • service duration;

address and contact details.

2.3. If the actual conditions materially differ from those specified on the Platform, the User may contact the Venue directly and, where necessary, contact iBron Support.

3. Rights and Obligations of the User

3.1. The User must:

provide an accurate name, telephone number and any other requested information;

  • carefully select the service, Professional, date and time;
  • arrive at the scheduled time without significant delay;
  • comply with the rules of the relevant Venue;
  • handle the Venue’s equipment, premises and property with care;
  • provide advance notice of any cancellation or rescheduling;
  • refrain from creating knowingly false or fictitious bookings;

present an identity document upon a reasonable request from the Venue, where this is necessary for the provision of the service or access to the booked facility;

in the event of a dispute, provide any available supporting evidence, including photographs, correspondence, receipts, notifications and other materials.

3.2. The User has the right to:

  • receive information about the Venue, Professional, service and booking conditions;

see the price of the service before submitting a booking request;

receive notification of the confirmation, cancellation or change in the status of the booking;

cancel or reschedule the booking where this is permitted under the Venue’s rules;

refuse the service if its actual conditions materially differ from those specified on the Platform;

contact iBron Support in the event of technical issues or disputes;

manage available bookings through the User’s personal account.

4. Rights and Obligations of the Venue, Professional or Service Provider

4.1. The Venue or Professional must:

  • provide accurate and up-to-date information about the services;
  • promptly confirm or decline incoming booking requests;

provide the service on the confirmed date and at the confirmed time;

provide the selected Professional, facility, workstation or equipment, or agree any replacement with the User in advance;

  • inform the User in advance of any important attendance requirements;

specify the exact price of the service or the method used to calculate it;

  • maintain an up-to-date schedule;
  • promptly block unavailable dates and times;
  • notify the User of any cancellation or rescheduling;
  • respond to the User’s enquiries and requests from iBron Support;

comply with the laws of the Republic of Uzbekistan when providing services.

4.2. The Venue or Professional has the right to:

  • confirm or decline a booking request;
  • offer the User a different time, Professional or facility;
  • cancel the booking where there is a justified reason;

refuse to provide a service to a User who violates the Venue’s rules, behaves aggressively or poses a threat to employees, customers or property;

  • cancel the booking if the User is significantly late;

require payment for the service in accordance with the stated conditions;

establish its own cancellation, rescheduling, prepayment and refund policies, provided that such policies are communicated to the User before the booking is confirmed.

5. Booking Confirmation

5.1. After a booking request has been submitted, the booking is assigned the relevant status.

5.2. Depending on the Venue’s settings, a booking may:

  • be confirmed automatically;
  • await confirmation by the Venue;
  • be declined;
  • be cancelled;

be marked as completed after the service has been provided.

5.3. Until confirmation is received, the User must not consider the selected time to be finally reserved.

5.4. Confirmation may be sent:

  • through the iBron website;
  • through the User’s personal account;
  • by SMS;
  • through a Telegram bot;

by any other available method.

5.5. The User is responsible for independently monitoring the booking status and ensuring that the provided contact details are correct.

6. Cancellation and Rescheduling by the User

6.1. The User may cancel or reschedule a booking in accordance with the rules of the selected Venue.

6.2. The Venue may establish a free cancellation period, for example, requiring cancellation no later than a specified number of hours before the scheduled time.

6.3. If the User:

  • fails to arrive at the scheduled time;
  • arrives significantly late;
  • cancels the booking after the applicable cancellation deadline;
  • repeatedly creates bookings and fails to attend,

the Venue may apply restrictions established by the Venue, including refusing future bookings or retaining any prepayment made, provided that such conditions were communicated in advance.

6.4. Users are advised to cancel any booking they no longer require as early as possible so that the time slot may become available to other customers.

7. Cancellation by the Venue or Professional

7.1. The Venue or Professional may cancel a confirmed booking only where there is a justified reason, including:

  • illness or absence of the Professional;
  • technical malfunction of equipment;
  • an emergency;
  • inability to provide the selected facility;
  • closure of the Venue;

other circumstances preventing the service from being provided.

7.2. In the event of cancellation, the Venue must promptly notify the User and, where possible, offer:

  • another time;
  • another Professional;
  • a similar service;

another bookable facility.

7.3. If the User has made a prepayment directly to the Venue or through an available payment method, the refund procedure shall be determined by the payment conditions, the Venue’s rules and applicable law.

7.4. iBron may take repeated unjustified cancellations into account when determining the Venue’s status, its visibility on the Platform and its eligibility to continue using the service.

8. Payment for Services

8.1. The price of the service, rental or booking is displayed on the page of the relevant Venue.

8.2. Depending on the available payment methods, the User may pay for services:

  • directly at the Venue;
  • in cash;
  • by bank card;
  • through a connected payment system;
  • by full or partial prepayment;

by another method agreed with the Venue.

8.3. If online payment is not available on the Platform, creation of a booking does not mean that the service has been paid for.

8.4. If the displayed price is preliminary or depends on the scope of work, the final price must be agreed between the User and the Venue before the service begins.

8.5. Additional services selected directly at the Venue shall be paid for separately.

8.6. iBron shall not be responsible for payments made by the User outside the payment methods provided through the Platform, including payments transferred to the personal bank card of an unknown person.

9. Specific Rules for Certain Booking Categories

9.1. Services Provided by Professionals and Salons

When booking the services of a hairdresser, barber, manicure specialist, massage therapist or another Professional, the User selects the service, Professional, date and time.

The actual duration and price may change if the scope of the service changes, subject to the User’s agreement.

9.2. Computer Clubs

When booking a computer club, the User may select the number of places, the type of computer, the tariff and the duration of the session.

The User must comply with the club’s rules and shall be responsible for any damage caused to the equipment in accordance with the Venue’s rules and applicable law.

9.3. Billiard Clubs

When booking a billiard table, the User selects the date, time, duration and available table.

The booking period may be extended only if there is no subsequent booking and with the consent of the Venue’s administration.

9.4. Padel Courts and Sports Facilities

When booking a court or sports facility, the User must comply with the scheduled start and end times of the rental period.

Equipment rental, coaching services and additional options may be charged separately if they are not included in the price.

9.5. Rental of Places and Equipment

If a specific place, table, station, room or item of equipment is booked, the Venue must provide a facility of the relevant type or agree an equivalent replacement with the User.

10. Late Arrival and No-Show

10.1. The User must arrive at the scheduled time.

10.2. If the User arrives late, the duration of the service may be reduced without a reduction in price where other bookings are scheduled after the User’s booking.

10.3. The Venue may cancel the booking after the applicable waiting period has expired.

10.4. The waiting period is determined by the Venue and may be displayed on the booking page.

10.5. A User’s failure to attend may be taken into account by the Platform in connection with the User’s future use of the service.

11. Resolution of Disputes

11.1. The User and the Venue must first attempt to resolve any dispute directly between themselves.

11.2. iBron Support may consider enquiries in the following circumstances:

  • the Venue has refused to provide a confirmed service;
  • the actual conditions materially differ from those specified;
  • a technical error occurred when creating or cancelling the booking;
  • the booking status is displayed incorrectly;

the parties are unable to establish the circumstances of the dispute independently.

11.3. The following materials may be required in order to consider an enquiry:

  • the booking number;
  • photographs;
  • screenshots;
  • correspondence;
  • receipts;
  • payment information;
  • notifications;

other supporting materials.

11.4. iBron shall consider the dispute on the basis of these Rules, the available technical data and the evidence provided.

11.5. iBron may request additional information and may refuse to consider an enquiry if the User or Venue fails to provide the information necessary to conduct the review.

11.6. iBron’s participation in the resolution of a dispute does not mean that iBron assumes the obligations of the Service Provider, unless otherwise expressly required by law or a separate agreement.

12. Liability of the Parties

12.1. The Venue or Professional shall be responsible for the quality, safety, legality and actual provision of the service.

12.2. The User shall be responsible for the accuracy of the information provided, compliance with the Venue’s rules and timely arrival.

12.3. iBron shall not be responsible for:

  • the quality of the service provided;
  • the actions of the Venue’s employees or representatives;

any change in price agreed between the User and the Venue;

any harm caused in connection with the provision of the service;

  • cancellation or rescheduling initiated by the Venue;
  • private arrangements made between the parties outside the Platform;

temporary unavailability of the service for technical reasons.

12.4. iBron may restrict or terminate the User’s or Venue’s access to the Platform in the event of:

  • fraud;
  • creation of fictitious bookings;
  • repeated no-shows;
  • provision of inaccurate information;
  • violation of the rights of other persons;
  • misuse of the Platform’s functions;

violation of the User Agreement.

13. Final Provisions

13.1. By using the booking function, the User confirms that they have read and agreed to these Rules.

13.2. By listing services and accepting bookings through iBron, the Venue also confirms its agreement to these Rules.

13.3. iBron may amend these Rules. A revised version shall take effect from the time it is published on the website, unless a different effective date is specified separately.

13.4. Any matters not governed by these Rules shall be governed by the iBron User Agreement and the laws of the Republic of Uzbekistan.
