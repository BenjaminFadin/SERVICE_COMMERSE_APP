1. Terms and Definitions

1.1. Platform

The iBron Platform is an information system owned by the Offeror and comprising the iBron.uz website, personal accounts, the business management dashboard, Telegram bot, software interfaces, databases, mobile applications and other digital solutions that enable users to search for Venues, view services, manage schedules and create Bookings.

1.2. User

A User is any individual or legal entity, individual entrepreneur or self-employed person who visits the Platform, registers on it or uses its functions.

A single User may simultaneously act as a Client, Partner, representative of a Venue or Professional.

1.3. Client

A Client is an individual who uses the Platform to search for, select and book services, appointment times, workstations, equipment, courts, tables, computers, premises or other facilities.

1.4. Partner

A Partner is a legal entity, individual entrepreneur, self-employed person, independent professional or other person who lawfully provides services and lists information about themselves, their Venue, Professionals, services or bookable facilities on the Platform.

1.5. Venue

A Venue is an organisation, commercial location, professional or facility available for search and booking through iBron, including:

  • hair salons;
  • barbershops;
  • beauty salons;
  • manicure and pedicure studios;
  • massage and SPA salons;
  • independent Professionals;
  • computer clubs;
  • billiard clubs;
  • padel courts;
  • sports facilities;
  • studios and premises;

other organisations and professionals operating by appointment or booking.

1.6. Professional

A Professional is a specialist, employee or person engaged by a Partner who provides a particular service to a Client.

A Professional may act independently or on behalf of a Venue.

1.7. Partner Service

A Partner Service is a service, rental, session, access to equipment, a workstation, court, table, computer, premises or another facility provided directly by the Partner to the Client.

iBron is not the provider of a Partner Service unless otherwise expressly stated on the checkout page.

1.8. Booking

A Booking is a request created through the Platform by a Client to reserve a specific service, date, time, Professional, place, item of equipment or facility.

1.9. Account

An Account is a User account created on the Platform that enables the User to access its personalised functions.

1.10. Business Account

A Business Account is a Partner’s account providing access to functions for managing a Venue, services, Professionals, schedules, Bookings, Client information and other business tools.

1.11. Subscription

A Subscription is access provided to a Partner, for a specified paid period, to the functions of the Business Account and other iBron digital tools.

1.12. Subscription Plan

A Subscription Plan is the combination of the price, validity period and conditions for providing a Subscription.

The Platform may offer payment periods of:

1 month;

3 months;

6 months;

12 months.

A discount may be provided when paying for a longer period.

1.13. Sponsored Placement

Sponsored Placement is an additional paid digital service under which a Venue’s or Partner’s profile may receive increased visibility on the Platform for a selected period.

Sponsored Placement may be marked with the words “Sponsored”, “Promoted”, “Recommended”, “Advertisement” or a similar label.

1.14. Content

Content means information posted on the Platform, including names, descriptions, photographs, images, logos, prices, schedules, lists of services, Professional profiles, reviews, contact details and other materials.

1.15. Payment Provider

A Payment Provider is a bank, payment organisation, payment system operator or other authorised payment service provider that processes online payments.

1.16. Booking Rules

The Booking Rules are a separate document published on the Platform that establishes the procedure for creating, confirming, cancelling and fulfilling Bookings.

The Booking Rules form an integral part of this Offer.

1.17. Privacy Policy

The Privacy Policy is a separate document that establishes the procedure for collecting, using, storing, transferring and protecting Users’ personal data.
